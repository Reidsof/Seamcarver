BROOKE'S SEAMCARVER

Seamcarver is a tool which takes in an image's individual pixels and computes its energy. It uses energy to determine which pixels are more important and deletes the lesser important pixels. Energy is computed by comparing a pixel's color to the average of it's surrounding pixels. It takes a row or column of this pixels across the image map, otherwise known as a seam.
